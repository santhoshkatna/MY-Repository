Our Boutique Advantage

Why 3 Acres Produces Better Guavas Than 300

In a world of sprawling industrial farms, our deliberate smallness is our greatest strength — and your platform's competitive edge.

01

Every Tree Gets Personal Attention

On a 3-acre boutique orchard, we personally monitor every single tree through each growth stage. No overlooked branches, no missed stress signals, no skipped bio-input applications. This hands-on vigilance is simply impossible at industrial scale — and the difference shows in every fruit.

02

Ancient Bio-Inputs, Modern Precision

We exclusively nourish our orchard with Panchagavya (a traditional fermented cattle-product bio-stimulant) and Jeevamrutam (a living microbial soil activator). These formulations build extraordinary soil health, producing fruits with exceptional natural sweetness, dense nutrition, and long-lasting freshness — with zero chemical residue.

03

Regional-First Means Freshness-First

We do not export internationally. Every fruit remains within the domestic supply chain. This means we harvest at the precise moment of peak maturity — not days early to survive long-distance shipping. Your platform receives Allahabad Safedas with maximum crunch, superior shelf life, and the authentic flavour that retail customers return for.

04

Precision Drip Irrigation Throughout

Our orchard runs on a calibrated drip irrigation system delivering exactly the right amount of water directly at the root zone. This conserves resources, prevents overwatering stress, and ensures consistent, uniform fruit sizing — a critical advantage for retail shelf presentation and SKU standardisation.
